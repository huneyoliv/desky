Features

Everything you need to stay focused.

⏱ Study Timer

Standard and Pomodoro modes with subject tracking and offline sync.

👥 Study Groups

Live group feed, group chat, real-time member status and social nudges.

📊 Rankings

Global, regional and category leaderboards with activity heatmap.

📅 Planner

D-Day countdowns, weekly timetable and smart to-do list.

🃏 Flashcards & PDF

Spaced repetition decks with a built-in PDF reader.

🔔 Notifications

Persistent inbox with group alerts and challenge updates.
